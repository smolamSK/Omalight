The control popup

What each control does

Six parts on one card. Every change lands on the light as you drag.

  1. 1

    Power switch

    Turns the light on or off.

  2. 2

    Status line

    On or Off, with the brightness and warmth in use.

  3. 3

    Brightness

    How strong the light is, from 0 to 100 percent.

  4. 4

    Width

    How thick the edge band is, from 4 to 120 pixels.

  5. 5

    Warmth

    From 2700 K, a warm amber, to 6500 K, a cool white.

  6. 6

    Hint

    Tells you what you can do next.
