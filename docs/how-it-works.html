How it works

One click turns the light on across every screen

01

Bottom bar icon

Click the lightbulb, or press SUPER ALT P.

02

Control popup

Opens above the icon with the power switch and three sliders.

03

Service

Holds the state, and saves your settings to a file.

04

Edge light

A click through glow along each screen edge.

What it touches on your system

  • Brightness: sets the screen to 100 percent when the light turns on.
  • Theme: matches the popup to your Omarchy colours.
  • Settings: saved to ~/.config/omarchy/omalight/settings.json, and nothing else.
  • Network and camera: none. No video or audio is read.

Keyboard shortcuts

SUPER ALT E

Turn the light on or off

SUPER ALT P

Open or close the popup
